Short definitions of the vocabulary used across the documentation. Each entry links to the page
that treats it in full where one exists.

## A to C

**Alpha.** The part of a return not explained by exposure to a benchmark. In a research setting it
is an estimated intercept, with all the estimation error that implies.

**Autocorrelation.** The correlation of a series with a lagged copy of itself. Positive
autocorrelation makes a sample look smoother than it is and understates risk.

**Conditional volatility.** Volatility given the information available up to now - the quantity a
GARCH model estimates. Contrasted with unconditional volatility, the long-run average.

## D to H

**Drawdown.** The fall from a running peak, as a percentage. See [Risk metrics
glossary](/docs/risk-metrics-glossary).

**Heteroskedasticity.** A variance that is not constant over time. Volatility clustering is a
specific, persistent form of it.

**Hold-out sample.** Data set aside and not used during estimation, so that a result can be scored
on data it has never seen.

## L to R

**Long memory.** Dependence that decays slowly, so a shock is still felt after many periods.

**Overfitting.** Fitting the noise in a sample rather than the structure. It shows up as excellent
in-sample performance and poor out-of-sample performance. See [Backtesting and
validation](/docs/backtesting-and-validation).

**Persistence.** In a GARCH model, the sum of the reaction and memory coefficients. Close to one
means shocks decay slowly.

## S to Z

**Stationarity.** A property of a series whose mean, variance and autocovariance do not depend on
the calendar. Most classical results assume it.

**Value at risk.** A loss threshold exceeded with a stated probability over a stated horizon. See
[Risk metrics glossary](/docs/risk-metrics-glossary).

**Volatility clustering.** The tendency of large changes to be followed by large changes. The
stylised fact that motivates the whole GARCH family.

**Walk-forward validation.** Re-fitting a model on a moving window and scoring it out of sample at
each step.
